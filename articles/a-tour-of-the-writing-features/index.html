A Tour of the Writing Features

Diagrams, maths, videos, light and dark pictures, and glossary hints — everything you can put inside an article.

ASRBMY Oct 12, 2026 1 min read
  • writing
  • markdown
  • tutorials
Table of Contents
  1. Diagrams
  2. Maths
  3. Videos
  4. Pictures that follow the theme
  5. Hints for hard words

This article shows what you can add to a page beyond plain text. Every feature works with ordinary Markdown (definition: A plain-text way of writing headings, lists and links with simple symbols, so a file stays readable anywhere and can be turned into a web page.), and none of them needs a plugin.

Diagrams#

Write the diagram as text in a mermaid block and it is drawn for you, in the colours of the current theme.

Maths#

Put a formula between dollar signs, such as eiπ+1=0e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0, or on its own lines for a larger display:

−b±b2−4ac2a\frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}

Prices like $5 and $10 stay as ordinary text.

Videos#

A video stays a plain button until you press it, so nothing is loaded from a video site by default.

Pictures that follow the theme#

If a picture named diagram-light.svg has a twin called diagram-light.dark.svg, the right one is shown in light or dark mode.

Hints for hard words#

The first mention of a term from the glossary, such as Markdown or RSS (definition: A standard format that lets a reader app collect new articles from many sites in one place, without accounts or email.), shows its meaning when you hover over it or tab to it.
